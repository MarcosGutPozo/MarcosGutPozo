# Marcos Gutiérrez-del-Pozo
### PhD Researcher in Mathematics & Artificial Intelligence | University of Seville

I am currently pursuing a PhD in Applied Mathematics at the University of Sevilla, within the Department of Applied Mathematics I, working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and applied mathematics, particularly topology and geometry. My research focuses on developing mathematically grounded methods to study the internal representations, interpretability, and generalization properties of high-dimensional AI models.
---

## 🔬 Research Papers

### 1. Topological Layer Selection for Activation Steering in LLMs

**Authors:** Tuc Nguyen, Marcos Gutiérrez-del-Pozo, Eduardo Paluzo-Hidalgo, Yifan Hu, Adaku Uchendu and Thai Le.

**Status:** Preprint (under review), October 2026.

Developed a framework for identifying optimal activation steering layers in large language models. The approach leverages persistent homology to quantify the geometric structure of activation displacements, providing a principled criterion for layer selection.

- 📄 [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.01493)

### 2. Persistent Entropy as a Detector of Phase Transitions

**Authors:** Marcos Gutiérrez-del-Pozo, Matteo Rucco and Eduardo Paluzo-Hidalgo.

**Status:** Preprint (under review), September 2026.

Developed a model-agnostic framework based on persistent entropy to detect dispersion–condensation transitions in convolutional neural networks. The method identifies structural changes in the distribution of learned filters, providing insights into the evolution and interpretability of neural network representations. Validated on MNIST and CIFAR-10.
